{site} ranks every professional football club in Turkey, from the Süper Lig down to the 3. Lig, on a single Elo scale. Each club's page explains, match by match, why and by how much its rating changed. The full method is on [How the ratings work](/en/metodoloji/).

Ratings include the matches of {date} (model {model}).

## Who makes it?

The site is made by Egemen Derbedek. It is a personal, non-commercial project: no ads, no accounts, no paid content. It has no link to the Turkish Football Federation, UEFA or any club, and the ranking here is not the official league table.

## Code and data

The code that collects the data and computes the ratings, the raw data and this site's code are public on [GitHub]({repo}). Where the data comes from is on the [Sources](/en/kaynaklar/) page.

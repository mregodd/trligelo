## Domestic matches and clubs

Results of the Süper Lig, 1. Lig, 2. Lig, 3. Lig, play-offs, Turkish Cup and Super Cup, the league tables, club names and club logos come from the [Türkiye Futbol Federasyonu](https://www.tff.org) (Turkish Football Federation, tff.org). As its terms require, they are used non-commercially and with the source named. Club logos are the trademarks of their clubs.

## European matches and UEFA coefficients

Turkish clubs' Champions League, Europa League and Conference League matches and their opponents' UEFA club coefficients come from the English [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org) competition pages. That content is licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and data derived from it is under the same terms.

European matches are also checked against [openfootball](https://github.com/openfootball) (public domain, CC0).

## The ratings

The ratings are computed from this data with the [Elo method](/en/metodoloji/). Raw data, ratings and code are on [GitHub]({repo}).

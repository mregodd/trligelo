## In short

This site uses no cookies. There are no accounts, forms or comments; no personal data such as a name or an email address is asked for or collected.

## Visit statistics

To see how much the site is used, it runs Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and stores no identifier on your device. It counts page views, which site visitors came from, their country and device type as totals; it does not recognise individual visitors or follow them across sites. Details in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, Cloudflare may keep technical logs of the requests it serves.

## Theme choice

Your light or dark theme choice is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and is never sent to the site.
